# Future UX enhancements - not implemented in Stage 8A.5

## Premium achievement reveal

Aim for the brief satisfaction of a well-made physical collectible, with the care seen in Apple Watch Fitness award presentation as inspiration. Use entirely original Gluca artwork and motion; do not copy Apple medals, branding, layouts or animations.

A future concept could combine a short medal reveal, slight rotation/visual depth, restrained shimmer, optional device haptic and a brief settling transition. Keep the sequence short, dismissible and queued until the user leaves Story Studio or another active editor. Reduced-motion users receive a static presentation; haptics and sounds must be optional. Never include diagnosis dates, glucose values or health-success claims. Only genuinely new eligible awards may trigger it. Do not build streak guilt, rarity or rank language.

Claude design note: explore original motion storyboards and material treatments against the current SVG medal system. Keep equal icon dimensions and legible small glyphs. User review, accessible motion/haptic settings and real-device checks precede implementation. Stage 8A.5 uses only a 180ms opacity/6px entrance and 140ms exit, not 3D or shimmer.

## Advanced touch-friendly Story Studio editing

Preserve the existing shared normalized scene/export model. Future work may improve handle hit areas, pinch anchoring, center/safe-margin snapping, reachable selection bounds and independently selectable chart/stat groups where appropriate. Reserve rotation without enabling accidental distortion. Maintain aspect ratio, preview/export coordinate fidelity, keyboard controls, accessible alternatives and real alpha. Validate on physical phones with scrolling, safe areas and the keyboard before adding complexity.

Claude design note: prototype a compact selection toolbar and clear gesture affordances using existing typography/tokens. Avoid permanently visible direction pads. Do not replace the renderer or reimplement glucose calculations.

## Publishing production gates

Before public hosting: mature account recovery/deletion and age/privacy policies, HTTPS/secure cookies, key rotation/backups/retention, per-account storage quotas, abuse/moderation/reporting, bounded pagination and operational monitoring without health payloads. Followers-only requires a real relationship model and authorization tests on both feed and image reads. Native posting needs a verified authenticated media handoff. Durable offline retry needs an explicitly protected local storage/key-management design; do not save images or private captions in plaintext localStorage.

No Stage 8B work is authorized by this document.
