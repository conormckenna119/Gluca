# Validation plan

Goal: learn whether people with diabetes want to share graphics like this, before building more.

## Participants
8–12 adults with type 1 diabetes using Dexcom, mixed by: comfort sharing health info publicly, social platform used, G6/G7. Recruit via personal network and diabetes communities, with consent and no incentive tied to posting.

## Sessions (20 min, remote or in person, on their own phone)
1. Ask: have you ever shared glucose data? Where, why not? (no prompt of the product yet)
2. Give them the link. Ask them to try demo data, then (only if they choose) their own Clarity CSV on their device. We never see or collect the file.
3. Observe: time to first graphic, which template, whether they open Customize, what they change, hesitations around privacy.
4. Ask them to export. Ask where they would post it, what they would change first, what they would never show.
5. Questions: Does it feel like *your* day? Would you post it? What would make you uncomfortable? Would you want to see others'?

## Assumptions to test
| # | Assumption | Evidence that would support it |
| - | --- | --- |
| A1 | People will import their own file, not only demo | ≥60% of sessions import |
| A2 | The default graphic is good enough to export without edits | ≥50% export with no customisation |
| A3 | Humour templates feel like "theirs", not off-putting | Mixed tones are chosen; no one reports feeling mocked |
| A4 | The "real side" template feels honest rather than shaming | Felt-represented ≥4/5 for that template |
| A5 | Users trust "nothing is uploaded" | Few hesitate at import; can explain it back |
| A6 | A Clarity file from the real app parses correctly | 100% of real files import or give a clear, correct error |
| A7 | Exports work in their target app (Stories, feed) | Posts look right; transparent behaves as expected |

## Safety review
Before any session: a clinician or diabetes educator reviews all preset copy for messages that could encourage unsafe management or stigma.

## Decision rule
Proceed to a community layer if A1, A2 and A4 hold and at least a third of participants say they would post. Otherwise iterate templates or reconsider.
